ABOUT US

Education, Women Empowerment, And Environmental And Animal Welfar

InAmigos Foundation is a registered Section 8 non-profit organization in India focused on social welfare, youth development, and community empowerment. It runs six core initiatives spanning food distribution, education, animal welfare, women's empowerment, environmental conservation, and skill-building internships.

It is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization, licensed by the Central Government. It has its base at Chhattisgarh. It holds 80G & 12A certifications, ensuring transparency, accountability and tax-exempt benefits for donors.

🎯 Goal

InAmigos Foundation empowers underprivileged communities through education, essential support, and sustainable development.

50,000+ meals • 20,000+ saplings • 30,000+ interns empowered.
